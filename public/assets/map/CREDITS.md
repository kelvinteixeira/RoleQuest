# Map asset credits

All third-party models in `props/` are CC0 (public domain). Attribution is not
required, but given gladly.

| Files | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Files without the `q_` prefix (e.g. `tree_oak.glb`, `bridge_wood.glb`, `cliff_cave_rock.glb`) | **Nature Kit 2.1** by Kenney — https://kenney.nl · mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/nature-kit | CC0 1.0 |
| Files with the `q_` prefix (e.g. `q_CommonTree_1.glb`) | **Stylized Nature MegaKit (Standard)** by Quaternius — https://quaternius.com · mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-nature-megakit | CC0 1.0 |

Quaternius models were repacked to GLB with textures resized to 512 px WebP
(`@gltf-transform/cli optimize --texture-compress webp --texture-size 512`).
Terrain tiles are generated procedurally by Role Quest and are not third-party assets.
